Research

Asteroid Photometry · NSF REU

During a 10-week Physics & Astronomy REU at East Texas A&M University, I collected and analyzed astronomical data to study asteroid rotation.

June–August 2026
  • Calibrated more than 1,300 CCD images in MaxIm DL using dark, flat, and bias frames.
  • Used differential photometry and Fourier analysis in MPO Canopus to derive lightcurves and rotational periods for six asteroids.
  • Generated 3D shape estimates and coauthored a first-author manuscript.

Manuscript submitted: Mayo, C., and Montgomery, K., “Rotational Periods of Six Asteroids with Lightcurves,” 2026, Minor Planet Bulletin. Not yet published.
