## Problem

A four-axis printer with rotational non-planar extrusion needs coordinated motion, reliable power distribution, and a clear electrical architecture connecting motors, drivers, and limit endstops.

## My role

I am working on electrical and firmware development within TURTLE Robotics' PRNT project. My focus is prototyping the electrical architecture and embedded control system.

## Approach

- Develop C/C++ firmware for multi-axis stepper actuation, coordinated kinematics, and limit-endstop interfaces.
- Design electrical schematics and system wiring diagrams in KiCad.
- Plan DC power distribution and harness routing alongside the motion-control hardware.

## Current status

This project is **in progress**. Electrical prototyping and firmware development are ongoing; this case study describes work underway, not a completed or validated printer. Performance measurements and finished-system results are not yet reported here.
