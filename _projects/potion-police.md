## Problem

The EOG challenge at HackUTD 2025 called for making operational flow and shipment data easier to inspect. Our team used the event's potion-and-cauldron theme to build a monitoring dashboard for identifying discrepancies.

## My role

I worked as a developer and project manager, coordinating the team's work and delivery during the hackathon. This was a collaborative project with Calla Lee and Anushka Gupta; the dashboard's features are team outcomes, not solely my work.

## Approach

- Retrieve challenge data with Python and Requests, then process it with Pandas.
- Build an interactive Streamlit dashboard with level graphs, maps, and shipment-ticket inspection.
- Check ticket and flow discrepancies and visualize mismatches to support investigation.
- Provide minute-level historical playback to help review transactions over time.

## Results and scope

We delivered an interactive hackathon prototype. The public project gallery shows tank levels, ticket views, graphs, a map, and a daily mismatch heatmap. It is not evidence of a production deployment or a measured reduction in shipment losses.

Forecasting and optimized courier routes were listed as **future work**, not implemented capabilities.
